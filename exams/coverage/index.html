This inventory separates question coverage from worked-solution coverage. CSAT Series A spans the full Q1-80 question range across two pages, but its explanation coverage is still partial because Q62 and Q72 are key-only. Several other sets cover selected questions only. This is not a full library of papers for any exam.

2. Original lessons with practice (not PYQ)

Fifteen lesson pages: Economy 1-2, Polity 1, Geography 1-2, Science 1, Rajasthan 1-2, Quantitative Aptitude 1-3, Data Interpretation 1, Reasoning 1-2 and English 1. They are general aptitude, reasoning, English and subject notes with practice questions. They carry no verified previous-year questions.

3. Exam details

The SSC and IBPS reference page gives the SSC CGL 2026 notice and IBPS PO/MT-XVI notification: pattern, marking and syllabus headings. It is only an SSC/IBPS reference. Railway exam-pattern figures are not published here, and that page is not a railway pattern reference.

4. Previous-year papers not covered

  • SSC: the public Previous Year Question Paper page listed "No Data Available" when viewed on 26 Nov 2026. SSC notices describe papers and keys as available by candidate login for personal use.
  • Banks: the IBPS CRP FAQ and the SBI PO recruitment FAQ each say the body does not provide the question paper, answer sheet or key. This covers only those two pages. Other bank exams are not covered. IBPS's PO information handout has illustrative sample questions, which are not previous papers.
  • Railway: no official previous-year paper and key pair is included here. Railway exam-pattern details are pending.
  • State exams: only TNPSC is covered. Other state exams are not covered here.
  • Also not covered: CUET, NDA/CDS/CAPF and ESE. UPSC 2022 and 2024-2026 papers are not included.

5. What to use

For SSC and bank preparation, use the SSC/IBPS exam-details page and the original lessons. Railway students can use the original general practice lessons, but railway exam-pattern details are still pending. For real previous-paper practice, use the six PYQ pages above. This remains a bounded inventory of verified paper pages and original lessons, not a complete exam-paper library.
