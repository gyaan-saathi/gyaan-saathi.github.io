Notice-based structure

SSC CHSL 2026: exam structure (as per the official notice)

1. Tier-I (computer based, objective)

PartSubjectQuestionsMarks
IEnglish Language (Basic Knowledge)2550
IIGeneral Intelligence2550
IIIQuantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic Skill)2550
IVGeneral Awareness2550
  • Time: 60 minutes for all four parts, with a sectional timer of 15 minutes per subject (para 13.8).
  • Candidates eligible for a scribe under paras 7.1 to 7.3: 80 minutes, 20 minutes per subject.
  • Objective multiple-choice only. Part II to IV questions are set in English, Hindi and the language chosen in the application form (para 13.8.1). Part I (English) is not in that list.
  • Negative marking: 0.50 marks for each wrong answer (para 13.8.2).

2. Tier-II (two sessions on the same day)

Session-I is 2 hours 15 minutes. Session-II is the skill/typing test (paras 13.9.2 to 13.9.8).

SectionSubjectQuestionsMarksTime
I-AMathematical Abilities30901 hour, sectional timer 30 minutes each
I-BReasoning and General Intelligence3090
II-AEnglish Language and Comprehension401201 hour, sectional timer 40 and 20 minutes
II-BGeneral Awareness2060
IIIComputer Knowledge Test154515 minutes
  • Scribe-eligible candidates get extra time as printed in the notice table (for example 1 hour 20 minutes per section for Sections I and II, 20 minutes for Section III).
  • It is mandatory to qualify all sections of Tier-II (para 13.9.9).
  • Negative marking: 1 mark for each wrong answer in Sections I, II and III (para 13.9.11).
  • Section III (Computer Knowledge) is mandatory but qualifying in nature (para 13.9.12).
  • Section-II Part A (English) is not set in other languages (para 13.9.10).

3. Skill test / typing test (Section IV, qualifying)

  • Data Entry Operator: skill test is mandatory for every DEO. Posts in the departments named at para 8.1: 15,000 key depressions per hour, 15 minutes, about 3,700 to 4,000 key depressions of English text. Other DEO posts: 8,000 key depressions per hour, 15 minutes, about 2,000 to 2,200 key depressions (para 13.9.13.6).
  • LDC/JSA typing test: 10 minutes, medium Hindi or English chosen in the application form and final. English 35 words per minute, Hindi 30 words per minute. The notice says these are about 10,500 and 9,000 key depressions per hour (para 13.9.13.7).
  • Hindi typing must be in Mangal font only, other fonts lead to non-qualification (para 13.9.13.7 viii).
  • Scribe-eligible candidates get 5 extra minutes. Medical-certificate exemption rules for typing are in para 13.9.13.7 vii and the annexures.

4. Indicative syllabus headings (as listed in the notice)

Tier-I (para 13.10)

  • English: spot the error, fill in the blanks, synonyms/homonyms, antonyms, spelling, idioms and phrases, one word substitution, sentence improvement, active/passive voice, direct/indirect narration, shuffling of sentence parts and sentences, cloze passage, comprehension passage.
  • General Intelligence: verbal and non-verbal. Analogy, classification, series, Venn diagrams, coding-decoding, drawing inferences, pattern folding and unfolding, embedded figures, number operations, critical thinking and more.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: number systems, percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, partnership, mixture and alligation, time and distance, time and work, basic algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry (simple), statistical charts.
  • General Awareness: current events and everyday science, India and its neighbours: history, culture, geography, economic scene, general policy, scientific research.

Tier-II (para 13.11)

  • Mathematical Abilities: same arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration and trigonometry headings, with statistics and probability (mean, median, mode, standard deviation, simple probability) in place of charts only.
  • Reasoning and General Intelligence, and General Awareness: same headings as Tier-I.
  • English Language and Comprehension: Tier-I headings plus vocabulary and grammar. Comprehension uses two or more paragraphs, one simple (book or story) and one on current affairs (editorial or report).
  • Computer Knowledge: computer basics, Windows and MS Office basics (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), internet and email use. See para 13.11.5 for the full list.

These are the notice's headings, not a study plan and not a claim that any topic is covered on this website.

हिंदी सार

सरल अनुवाद, official शब्द नहीं

SSC CHSL 2026: परीक्षा का ढाँचा

Tier-I: चार भाग, हर भाग में 25 प्रश्न और 50 अंक: अंग्रेज़ी, सामान्य बुद्धि, मात्रात्मक योग्यता, सामान्य जागरूकता। कुल समय 60 मिनट, हर विषय का अलग 15 मिनट का टाइमर। हर गलत उत्तर पर 0.50 अंक कटेंगे।

Tier-II: एक ही दिन दो सत्र। पहला सत्र: गणितीय योग्यता 30 प्रश्न, तर्क 30 प्रश्न, अंग्रेज़ी 40 प्रश्न, सामान्य जागरूकता 20 प्रश्न, कंप्यूटर ज्ञान 15 प्रश्न। पहले तीन भागों में हर गलत उत्तर पर 1 अंक कटेगा। सभी भाग पास करना ज़रूरी है। कंप्यूटर ज्ञान और typing/skill test केवल qualifying हैं, पर इनमें पास होना ज़रूरी है।

Typing / skill test: LDC/JSA के लिए 10 मिनट, अंग्रेज़ी में 35 और हिंदी में 30 शब्द प्रति मिनट। हिंदी typing केवल Mangal font में। DEO के लिए 15 मिनट का data-entry test, पद के अनुसार 8,000 या 15,000 key depressions प्रति घंटा।

तारीख: notice में Tier-I और Tier-II की तारीख "बाद में सूचित" लिखी है। ताज़ा schedule और corrigendum ssc.gov.in पर देखें।
