भाग 1: व्याख्या सहित

Q1 · WORKED

Indirect speech में बदलें: He said to Mr Y, "Mr Z will leave for his native place tomorrow."

(A) He told Mr Y that Mr Z will leave ... tomorrow (B) He told Mr Y that Mr Z left ... the next day (C) He told Mr Y that Mr Z would be leaving ... tomorrow (D) He told Mr Y that Mr Z would leave ... the next day

Official final key: D व्याख्या: इस प्रश्न में अपेक्षित बदलाव will से would और tomorrow से the next day है। दोनों बदलाव केवल D में हैं।

Q2 · WORKED

Positive degree लिखें: Ramesh is the strongest man in the village.

(A) Ramesh is a strong man in the village (B) Ramesh is a stronger man in the village (C) Ramesh is the stronger man in the village (D) No other man in the village is so strong as Ramesh

Official final key: D व्याख्या: Superlative से positive: 'No other ... is so + adjective + as'। A, B बराबर अर्थ नहीं देते, C comparative है।

Q3 · WORKED

रिक्त स्थान भरें: The air hostess told passengers to fasten seat belts during the ____.

(A) shut down (B) work out (C) take off (D) warm up

Official final key: C व्याख्या: विमान के take off के समय seat belt बाँधी जाती है। बाकी phrases उड़ान का कोई चरण नहीं बताते।

Q4 · WORKED

किन वाक्यों में gerund है? (a) They began playing (b) They began to play (c) We prefer to take tea (d) We prefer taking tea

(A) (a),(b) (B) (b),(c) (C) (a),(c) (D) (a),(d)

Official final key: D व्याख्या: Gerund वह -ing रूप है जो noun की तरह प्रयोग हो। (a) का playing और (d) का taking gerund हैं; (b), (c) में to-infinitive है।

Q5 · WORKED

उपयुक्त infinitive भरें: He is supposed ____ for his examinations now, but he is wasting his time.

(A) have been studied (B) to be studying (C) to study (D) has been studied

Official final key: B व्याख्या: 'is supposed to' के बाद to-infinitive आता है। जो काम अभी हो रहा होना चाहिए, उसके लिए continuous रूप 'to be studying' आता है। A और D infinitive रूप नहीं हैं।

Q6 · WORKED

Regular verb चुनें: Both the officer and the worker ____ the affected areas.

(A) forgot (B) visited (C) went (D) saw

Official final key: B व्याख्या: Regular verb past में -ed लेता है। forgot, went, saw irregular हैं; visited regular है।

Q8 · WORKED

'The Mission is accomplished' का negative रूप।

(A) The mission is not over (B) The mission is not finished (C) The mission is not completed (D) The mission is not accomplished

Official final key: D व्याख्या: Negative बनाने के लिए 'not' जोड़ें और वही participle रखें: 'is not accomplished'।

Q11 · WORKED

Active form: "Our army has been defeated by the enemy."

(A) The enemy was defeated our army (B) The enemy was defeated by our army (C) The enemy has defeated our army (D) The enemy had defeated our army

Official final key: C व्याख्या: Passive 'has been defeated' (present perfect) का active 'has defeated' होता है, agent subject बनता है।

Q12 · WORKED

Tense: I got up early and ____ a shower.

(A) have (B) am having (C) had (D) was having

Official final key: C व्याख्या: 'got up' simple past है, इसलिए मेल खाने वाली क्रिया 'had' है (had a shower)। 'have' और 'am having' present रूप हैं। 'was having' पृष्ठभूमि की क्रिया बताता है, 'got up and ...' क्रम में नहीं बैठता।

Q13 · WORKED

कौन से British-American शब्द जोड़े सही हैं? (1) Spanner-Wrench (2) Hoover-Nonsense (3) Bollocks-Vacuum (4) Hairslide-Barrette

(A) (1) and (2) (B) (1) and (4) (C) (2) and (3) (D) (3) and (4)

Official final key: B व्याख्या: Spanner का अमेरिकी रूप wrench और hairslide का barrette है, इसलिए (1) और (4) सही जोड़े हैं। (2) और (3) में अर्थ आपस में बदल गए हैं: Hoover ब्रिटिश अंग्रेज़ी में vacuum cleaner है, nonsense नहीं।

Q14 · WORKED

PDA का पूरा रूप:

(A) Personal Digital Assistant (B) Personal Development Agency (C) Personal Data Authority (D) Personal Data Array

Official final key: A व्याख्या: PDA का पूरा रूप Personal Digital Assistant है, यह organiser की तरह इस्तेमाल होने वाला छोटा हाथ का उपकरण है।

Q17 · WORKED

Idioms मिलाएँ: Fall out, Fall for, Fall flat, Fall foul of

(A) 4 3 1 2 (B) 2 4 3 1 (C) 1 3 4 2 (D) 2 3 1 4

Official final key: A व्याख्या: Fall out = झगड़ना (4)। Fall for = आकर्षण में आ जाना (3)। Fall flat = ढह जाना, विफल होना (1)। Fall foul of = टकराव में आना (2)। यानी 4, 3, 1, 2।

Q18 · WORKED

'It rains cats and dogs' में 'cats and dogs' का उपयुक्त शब्द।

(A) Strongly (B) Heavily (C) Speedily (D) Fast

Official final key: B व्याख्या: 'It rains cats and dogs' का अर्थ बहुत तेज़ बारिश है। बारिश के लिए 'Heavily' ठीक बैठता है। Strongly, speedily और fast दूसरी चीज़ें बताते हैं।

Q19 · WORKED

पाँच शब्दों (robust, affluent, odious, agility, gaunt) के antonyms मिलाएँ।

(A) 1-c 2-e 3-a 4-b 5-d (B) 1-a 2-d 3-c 4-e 5-b (C) 1-d 2-e 3-a 4-b 5-c (D) 1-b 2-e 3-c 4-a 5-d

Official final key: C व्याख्या: विलोम: robust - weak (d), affluent - poor (e), odious - pleasant (a), agility - awkwardness (b), gaunt - portly (c)। यानी 1-d, 2-e, 3-a, 4-b, 5-c।

Q20 · WORKED

कौन से जोड़े गलत हैं? (1) Gregarious: लड़खड़ाकर चलने वाला (2) Glutton: बहुत खाने वाला (3) Groggy: लोगों के साथ रहना पसंद करने वाला (4) Gourmet: खाने का शौक रखने वाला

(A) (1) and (2) (B) (1) and (3) (C) (3) and (4) (D) (1) and (4)

Official final key: B व्याख्या: Gregarious का अर्थ मिलनसार है, पर (1) में उसे 'लड़खड़ाकर चलने वाला' लिखा है। Groggy का अर्थ अस्थिर या अर्धचेतन है, पर (3) में उसे 'लोगों के साथ रहना पसंद करने वाला' लिखा है। इसलिए (1) और (3) गलत जोड़े हैं। (2) और (4) ठीक हैं।

Q21 · WORKED

किसी phrase के दो तत्वों की अदला-बदली को क्या कहते हैं?

(A) Hypallage (B) Malapropism (C) Metaphor (D) Simile

Official final key: A व्याख्या: Stem की परिभाषा hypallage की है। Malapropism में मिलते-जुलते उच्चारण वाला गलत शब्द आता है। Metaphor और simile तुलनाएँ हैं।

Q24 · WORKED

Fox and grapes कहानी के पाँच वाक्य क्रम में लगाएँ: (a) पीछे हटकर कूदा, चूक गया (b) हार मानकर 'शायद खट्टे हैं' कहा (c) एक दोपहर लोमड़ी ने अंगूर देखे (d) फिर कोशिश, फिर विफल (e) 'प्यास बुझाने के लिए बढ़िया'

Q29 · WORKED

वह स्थिति जब बैंक से कर्ज़ लेना कठिन हो जाए (ब्याज दरें बढ़ने, आर्थिक मंदी में)।

Q1 · WORKED

Change to indirect speech: He said to Mr Y, "Mr Z will leave for his native place tomorrow."

(A) He told Mr Y that Mr Z will leave ... tomorrow (B) He told Mr Y that Mr Z left ... the next day (C) He told Mr Y that Mr Z would be leaving ... tomorrow (D) He told Mr Y that Mr Z would leave ... the next day

Official final key: D Explanation: In this question the expected change is will to would and tomorrow to the next day. Only D makes both changes.

Q2 · WORKED

Positive degree of: Ramesh is the strongest man in the village.

(A) Ramesh is a strong man in the village (B) Ramesh is a stronger man in the village (C) Ramesh is the stronger man in the village (D) No other man in the village is so strong as Ramesh

Official final key: D Explanation: Superlative to positive: 'No other ... is so + adjective + as'. A and B are not equivalent; C is comparative.

Q3 · WORKED

Fill the blank: The air hostess told passengers to fasten seat belts during the ____.

(A) shut down (B) work out (C) take off (D) warm up

Official final key: C Explanation: Seat belts are fastened while the aircraft is taking off. The other phrases do not describe a stage of a flight.

Q4 · WORKED

Which sentences contain gerunds? (a) They began playing (b) They began to play (c) We prefer to take tea (d) We prefer taking tea. Options pair these sentences.

(A) (a),(b) (B) (b),(c) (C) (a),(c) (D) (a),(d)

Official final key: D Explanation: A gerund is a verb in -ing form used as a noun (object here). 'playing' in (a) and 'taking' in (d) are gerunds; (b) and (c) use to-infinitives.

Q5 · WORKED

Fill with a suitable infinitive: He is supposed ____ for his examinations now, but he is wasting his time.

(A) have been studied (B) to be studying (C) to study (D) has been studied

Official final key: B Explanation: 'is supposed to' takes a to-infinitive. For something that should be happening now, the continuous form 'to be studying' is used. A and D are not infinitive forms.

Q6 · WORKED

Choose the regular verb: Both the officer and the worker ____ the affected areas.

(A) forgot (B) visited (C) went (D) saw

Official final key: B Explanation: Regular verbs add -ed for past. forgot, went and saw are irregular. visited is regular.

Q8 · WORKED

Negative form of 'The Mission is accomplished'.

(A) The mission is not over (B) The mission is not finished (C) The mission is not completed (D) The mission is not accomplished

Official final key: D Explanation: Negate by adding 'not' and keep the same participle: 'is not accomplished'.

Q11 · WORKED

Active form of: "Our army has been defeated by the enemy."

(A) The enemy was defeated our army (B) The enemy was defeated by our army (C) The enemy has defeated our army (D) The enemy had defeated our army

Official final key: C Explanation: Passive 'has been defeated' (present perfect) becomes active 'has defeated', with the agent as subject.

Q12 · WORKED

Tense: I got up early and ____ a shower.

(A) have (B) am having (C) had (D) was having

Official final key: C Explanation: 'got up' is simple past, so the matching verb is 'had' (had a shower). 'have' and 'am having' are present forms. 'was having' describes a background action and does not fit the sequence 'got up and ...'.

Q13 · WORKED

Which British and American word pairs are correct? (1) Spanner-Wrench (2) Hoover-Nonsense (3) Bollocks-Vacuum (4) Hairslide-Barrette

(A) (1) and (2) (B) (1) and (4) (C) (2) and (3) (D) (3) and (4)

Official final key: B Explanation: A spanner is the British word for a wrench, and a hairslide is the British word for a barrette, so (1) and (4) are correct pairs. In (2) and (3) the meanings are swapped: Hoover is the British word for a vacuum cleaner, not nonsense.

Q17 · WORKED
