TNPSC Group IV 2025, General English, questions one to thirty-nine. This is from the Commission's specimen booklet and final answer key for the exam of twelve July 2025. The questions here are short rewordings, not the paper.

Five ideas from the questions. One. Direct to indirect speech: in question one, the expected answer shows will becoming would and tomorrow becoming the next day. Two. Superlative to positive: no other man in the village is so strong as Ramesh. Three. A gerund is an ing word used as a noun. They began playing, and we prefer taking tea, use gerunds. To play and to take are infinitives. Four. Passive to active: has been defeated becomes has defeated, with the doer as the subject. Five. For passage questions, accept only statements the passage supports. A passage about a city with traffic, pollution and crowded transport supports negative effects of unchecked growth, and infrastructure under strain. It does not support a claim about preserving landmarks.

Eleven questions in the notes give only the answer printed in the official key, and no explanation is given. Question forty is left out because its stem is in Tamil.
